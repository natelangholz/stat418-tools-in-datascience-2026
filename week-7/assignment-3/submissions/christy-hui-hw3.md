## Predictors Used

The model uses two predictor variables to predict `mpg`:

- **`wt`** — vehicle weight in 1000 lbs
- **`hp`** — gross horsepower

Both are negatively correlated with fuel efficiency. The model achieved a test R² of ~0.79 and MAE of ~2.24 mpg.
