Short Note: I used `wt` and `hp` as predictors to predict `mpg`. The `wt` variable represents car weight in 1000 lbs, and `hp` represents gross horsepower. I chose these predictors because both are related to fuel efficiency: heavier cars and cars with higher horsepower often have lower miles per gallon.
